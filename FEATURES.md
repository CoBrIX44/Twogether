# FEATURES
 
Legend: P0 = MVP, P1 = v1.5, P2 = later
 
## 1. Camera and capture
- **Dual camera panels (P0)**: two side-by-side live panels labelled Person 1 / Person 2 (editable names optional). On one device with one camera, the feed is shown in both slots as two framing guides, or users choose "take turns" (Person 1 shots, then Person 2).
- **Countdown (P0)**: large animated 3, 2, 1, then flash and shutter sound.
- **3 rounds (P0)**: progress dots (1/3, 2/3, 3/3), short pause between rounds to change poses.
- **Mirror toggle (P1)**, **switch camera front/back (P1)**.
- **Retake a round (P1)**.
- **Timer length setting (P2)**: 3 / 5 / 10 s.
- **Pose prompts (P2)**: playful text like "kiss on cheek!" between rounds.
## 2. Remote Together mode (P1)
- Create room, share code/link, join
- Live partner video in the right panel
- Host-controlled shutter, synced countdown
- Connection status and reconnect
## 3. Strip templates
- **Classic strip 3x2 (P0)**: three rows, two photos each
- **Colour themes (P0)**: white, black, pink, lavender, cream, checkered
- **Caption and date (P0)**: bottom area of the strip
- **Extra layouts (P1)**: 2x2 grid, polaroid pair, heart frame, vertical single-column
## 4. Filters
- Original, Black & White, Pink, Lavender, Sepia, Warm, Cool, Film grain (P0 up to 6)
- Intensity slider (P1)
- Per-photo vs whole-strip toggle (P1)
- Live thumbnails of each filter on the actual shot
## 5. Mascots and accessories
- Tray with categories: Mascots, Hearts and stars, Hats and glasses, Text bubbles, Washi tape
- Tap to add, drag to move, pinch/handles to resize and rotate
- Layer controls: bring forward, send back, duplicate, delete, flip
- Snap to photo slots is optional; free placement is the default
- Undo / redo (P1)
- Emoji and custom text stickers with font choice (P1)
- User upload sticker (P2)
## 6. Export
- **PNG** (lossless), **JPG** (quality 92), **PDF** (strip sized, or A4/4x6 print layout)
- Filename: `duo-booth-YYYYMMDD.ext`
- Resolution choice: Standard / High (P1)
- Share sheet on mobile (P2)
## 7. Polish
- Cute onboarding tooltips, confetti on finish, sound toggle
- Dark/light UI, reduced motion mode
- Fully responsive, installable PWA (P2)
