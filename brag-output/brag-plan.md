# Twogether — /brag plan

**What it is:** a browser photo booth for two — couples and best friends — that shoots a 3-round, 3×2 photo strip, lets you decorate it, and exports JPG/PNG/PDF. Same device or two devices anywhere via a room code.
**Who it's for:** couples, best friends, long-distance pairs.
**What sets it apart:** the strip is made *together* (dual viewfinder, synced countdown, remote rooms), and photos never leave the browser.
**Hook:** the real 3-2-1 booth countdown over two empty viewfinders → flash → both panels filled.
**Punchline:** the strip "prints out" — no signup, no upload.
**Tone:** default (punchy, playful, clean), landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, 20 s.
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.

- Real assets: frame PNGs from `public/photobooths/` (cherry cream, pink bows, plaid heart, scalloped floral), stickers from `public/stickers/`. Real copy from LandingPage / ModeSelectModal / ExportScreen.
- **Excluded:** `kitty.png` frame (features a licensed character) — not used.
- **Placeholder people:** the strip photos are drawn illustrations of two fictional people, not real photos. Room code shown is illustrative.

## Music
120 BPM (beat = 0.5 s, bar = 2 s), F major, soft pop: kick/clap/hats, bass, bell-ish melody. Shutter + pops + camera beep in key, on the grid, mixed under the music.
