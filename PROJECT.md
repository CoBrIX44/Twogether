# PROJECT: Duo Booth (working title)
 
## One-liner
An online photo booth where two people, side by side on two camera screens, take a 3-shot strip together, then decorate it with filters and cute mascots and download it as JPG, PNG or PDF.
 
## Why it exists
Offline photo booths are a loved ritual for couples and friends, but long-distance pairs can't do it. Existing online booths are solo-only, ad-heavy, or look dated.
 
## Core idea
- Two live camera panels, side by side
- Shared 3-2-1 countdown, 3 rounds
- Result auto-laid out as a classic booth strip (3 rows x 2 photos)
- Customise: filters, frame colours, mascots, stickers, text
- Export: JPG, PNG, PDF
## Two ways to play
1. **Together mode (remote)**: each person on their own device, joined with a room code or link. Peer-to-peer video.
2. **Same-device mode**: one camera, two people in frame (or taking turns). Needed because not everyone has a partner online at the same time, and it is the simplest thing to build first.
## Target users
Couples (primary), long-distance couples, best friends, content creators making cute shareable strips.
 
## Principles
- Privacy first: photos never leave the browser unless the user chooses
- No signup needed
- Mobile-first (most people will use phones)
- Cute, warm, playful design
## Success looks like
A pair can go from opening the site to a downloaded decorated strip in under 3 minutes.
